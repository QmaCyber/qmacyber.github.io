Privacy Policy for “Pop the balloon!”

Effective date: September 29, 2026
Android app: “Pop the balloon!”, package com.babygames.balloonpop.
Publisher: Qma

In short
  • The game does not collect or share personal data. There is no sign-up and there are no accounts.
  • Progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows Google AdMob ads. They are non-personalized only and suitable for children, and the advertising ID is not used.
  • Ads can be removed with a one-time Google Play purchase in the parents' section. Google processes the payment details, and the game never receives them.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This policy explains how the game “Pop the balloon!” (the “game”) handles data. The game is made for children aged 2–5 who play under parental supervision. The game is developed and published by Qma (“we”).

2. What data the game collects

We do not collect, store on our servers or share any personal data of users, including children. We run no servers for this game.

  • No sign-up, no login and no forms asking for a name, email or phone number.
  • No analytics, behavioral tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • The game does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.
  • There are no chats, no communication between users and no content sharing.

3. What is stored on the device

To remember settings and progress, the game saves the following in the app's local storage on your device:

  • sound and voice settings (on or off);
  • the stickers collected in the album and the number of rounds played;
  • a flag showing that ads were removed by purchase.

This data never leaves the device and is not sent to us. To delete it, uninstall the game or clear the app's data in Android settings.

4. Voice

Words are spoken in English by your device's built-in text-to-speech engine (the Android “Text-to-speech” system feature). The game only passes it the word to say, such as “red” or “three”, and does not send anything to the internet itself. How the engine works depends on your device settings and is governed by its developer's policy.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows interstitial ads from Google AdMob. It is the only ad network in the game, with no other networks and no mediation. Ads appear only after a game round is finished, never during play and never at app launch.

All ad requests are configured for a child audience:

  • requests are tagged for child-directed treatment (COPPA) and as coming from users under the age of consent (GDPR);
  • only non-personalized ads are shown: they are not based on the user's interests, history or profile;
  • the maximum ad content rating is G (general audiences);
  • the Android advertising ID is not used: the AD_ID permission is removed from the app, and Privacy Sandbox APIs are not used.

To serve and count ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK on the device contacts Google's servers. Google may process technical data such as:

  • IP address, to estimate the general region (country or city, not precise location), choose suitable ads and prevent fraud;
  • device and app information, such as device model, Android version, language, app name and version, and identifiers not linked to the advertising ID (for example, App set ID);
  • ad interactions, such as impressions, taps and closing;
  • diagnostic data about the SDK's performance.

Google processes this data as an independent party, and we do not receive it. We only see anonymous aggregated statistics (number of impressions and revenue). Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How AdMob supports Google Play's Families policy: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431

6. “Remove ads” purchase

Ads can be removed with a one-time purchase in the parents' section. Google Play handles the payment. Your card number and other payment details are processed by Google under its privacy policy, and the game never sees or receives them. The game only receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play and stores an “ads removed” flag on the device. The “Restore purchases” button asks Google Play for purchases already made with your Google account, for example after reinstalling the game.

7. Parents' section

Purchases, purchase restoration and external links (including the link to this policy) are available only in the parents' section. It is protected by a parental gate: to enter, you tap three digits that are written out as words. The children's part of the game has no links to websites, no purchases and no prompts to buy anything.

8. App permissions

PermissionWhy

WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICEadded by the Android task scheduler (WorkManager) that the Google ads SDK uses to finish sending technical ad requests; the game itself does not use them
com.babygames.balloonpop.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSIONan internal AndroidX permission that prevents other apps from sending system messages to the game

To keep the screen on during a round, the game uses a system window flag, which needs no permission. System libraries may add service permissions they need to work. These give no access to personal data, contacts, camera, microphone or location. The advertising ID permission (AD_ID) and Privacy Sandbox permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*) are removed from the app.

9. Children and compliance

The game is made for children, so it is subject to the Google Play Families Policy. We aim to comply with:

  • the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA);
  • the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including Article 8 on children's data (GDPR-K);
  • the Google Play Families Policy: we use only an ads SDK self-certified for Families apps (Google AdMob), and ads are non-personalized and suitable for children;
  • applicable personal data laws in the countries where the game is distributed.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe the game has received a child's data, contact us (section 12) and we will look into it.

10. Parents' rights

We do not keep any data about you or your child, so there is nothing for us to provide, correct or delete on request. Data on the device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data. Data processed by Google can be managed as described in Google's privacy policy. You can contact us with questions at any time.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change affects how data is handled, we will also mention it in the update notes on Google Play.
